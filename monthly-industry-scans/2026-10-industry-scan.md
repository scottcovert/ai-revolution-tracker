# October 2026 — Monthly AI Use-Case Scan (31 industries)

Covered month: October 2026 (sources back to 2026-09-15). Generated 2026-11-01.
Taxonomy: the 31 current AI-Rev industries. Companies are filed under the industry their **ticker** maps to, not the one their sector name suggests.

## Summary

- **Industries with material change: 8 of 31** (7 DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL, 1 BIFURCATION). The other 23 are NO_CHANGE: either no deployed AI use case with a date, or only demand-side or unverified evidence.
- **Most consequential finding:** Alphabet's Q3 release shows Google Cloud revenue of $30.7B against $15.2B a year earlier and Cloud operating income of $11.1B against $3.6B, with quarterly capex of $51.4B against $24.0B.
- **Flag counts:** DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL 7 · BIFURCATION 1 · NO_CHANGE 23 · DISPLACEMENT_REAL 0 · ADOPTION_PLATEAU 0.
- **Verification:** a separate verifier fetched 35 claim groups. 26 were confirmed as written. 9 were corrected or narrowed (wording, scope, or a dropped sub-figure), 2 sub-figures were removed as not found in source, and 1 broken URL was replaced. None were dead after correction. Six confirmed figures rest on secondary relays of company results and are marked.
- **Coverage caveat:** this was a thin month for primary sources. Several large members (Apple, NVIDIA, Palantir, Salesforce, Booking, McDonald's, Marriott, Moderna) had not reported by 2026-11-01. "NO_CHANGE" means "nothing verified", not proven absence. Each entry states the share of the industry's market cap the evidence covers.

Evidence tiers: **[VERIFIED-PUBLIC]** a fetched page states the claim as written · **[VENDOR-CLAIM]** the only source is the seller or the company's own adoption metric · **[INFERRED]** secondary relay, or the AI link is our inference.

## By industry

### Energy — NO_CHANGE
Evidence covers XOM and CVX (about 23% of cap) and SLB. Chevron and Exxon Q3 releases contain no AI use case. SLB reports Digital ARR of $1.04B at Sept 30 and a sensor-based production-chemistry program that avoided $4.4M in net costs over nine months at an unnamed Permian operator [VERIFIED-PUBLIC, company figures, no go-live date, so not scored].
Announced, not deployed: SLB data-center cooling expansion (acquisition closing H1 2027); this is demand from AI buildout.

### Materials — NO_CHANGE
LIN, BHP, RIO, SCCO, NEM, FCX (about 33% of cap) opened; AI appears only as a demand driver (copper, electricity, semiconductor gases). No deployed automation or AI figure.
Announced, not deployed: Rio Tinto aluminium AI assistant (planned, secondary German-language source, unscored).

### Capital Goods — NO_CHANGE
GE Vernova (Oct 28): total backlog $197B; gas-turbine equipment backlog plus slot reservations 116 to 122 GW [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. Caterpillar (Oct 29): Power Generation sales $3,421M, up 30%, primarily data-center applications [VERIFIED-PUBLIC; no AI mention in the portion reviewed]. Both are demand for the industry's products, not AI deployed inside it. SPCX (30% of cap) seen only through press and not scored.

### Commercial & Professional Services — DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL (low confidence)
- RELX (RELX, mapped here), trading update 2026-10-22: Legal underlying revenue growth +10% year to date (group +7%), with the Law Firms & Corporate Legal segment attributing growth to adoption of its integrated AI-enabled legal research platform [VERIFIED-PUBLIC, company statement; no AI-specific revenue figure].
Evidence covers about 6% of cap. WM and RSG Q3 releases show no AI deployment.

### Transportation — NO_CHANGE
UPS 8-K (Oct 27): reduced operational workforce and closed buildings under its automation-led network program; about $1.8B of benefits through Sept 30. The release does not mention AI [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. FedEx call (Oct 28): data-center vertical supported 28% growth in international freight revenue [INFERRED: third-party transcript].
Announced, not deployed: FedEx robotic loaders with Dexterity at Hagerstown, to be proven "in the coming months".

### Automobiles & Components — DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL
- Tesla (TSLA, 64% of cap), Q3 update 2026-10-21: 1.74M active FSD (Supervised) subscriptions (1.04M a year earlier); "nearly two million cumulative unsupervised miles" [VERIFIED-PUBLIC, SEC exhibit]. Waymo is Alphabet (see Interactive Media) and Zoox is Amazon (see Consumer Discretionary); neither is evidence here.
Announced, not deployed: Dallas robotaxi expansion and customer-owned vehicles on the network (2027), from search summaries.

### Consumer Durables & Apparel — NO_CHANGE
Nike Q3 release opened, no AI deployment disclosed. No material activity found.

### Consumer Services — NO_CHANGE
Starbucks and Hilton releases opened, nothing disclosed. Booking, McDonald's, Marriott and Airbnb report after the window.

### Consumer Discretionary Distribution & Retail — BIFURCATION
- Amazon (AMZN, 55% of cap), Q3 release 2026-10-29: Alexa for Shopping "used by 400 million+ customers in the last 12 months, with customer interactions up 570% year-over-year"; Zoox operating in 12 U.S. cities [VERIFIED-PUBLIC, company-reported usage].
- DoorDash (DASH): CEO said third-party AI agents sent "maybe several hundred orders in total"; users still order mainly through DoorDash's own app [VERIFIED-PUBLIC via press report of the call].
First-party assistants scale; third-party agent commerce is negligible so far.
Announced, not deployed: Walmart Agent Profiles (November 2026; Walmart is food-retail), Home Depot agent-identification talks (no date).

### Food & Staples Retailing — NO_CHANGE
Walmart (56% of cap) has only announcements this month (Agent Profiles, launching November 2026). Its Sparky figures date from February and are out of window.

### Food, Beverage & Tobacco — NO_CHANGE
KO, PEP, PM, BUD, MDLZ Q3 releases opened; none discloses AI deployment.

### Household & Personal Products — NO_CHANGE
P&G and Colgate (about 59% of cap) and Unilever filings mention AI only in risk factors. P&G's roughly $2B restructuring is not linked to AI.

### Health Care Equipment & Services — NO_CHANGE
UnitedHealth Q3 release (2026-10-13) lists "operational agentification" among investments and describes Optum AI-enabled solutions, with no quantified figure [VERIFIED-PUBLIC; not scored]. Intuitive Surgical and Elevance releases show no AI deployment. Evidence covers a minority of cap.

### Big Pharma — NO_CHANGE
Lilly's Q3 release mentions AI only in forward-looking statements. Announced, not deployed: AstraZeneca–Insitro collaboration (aggregator source only, unverified; Insitro is private).

### Biotech (AI) — NO_CHANGE
Announced, not deployed: Sanofi selected a first candidate from its AI collaboration with Recursion, a $10M milestone, Phase 1 planned 2027 (press wire, 2026-10-15). Vertex, Regeneron and Moderna had no Q3 evidence in the window.

### Life Sciences Tools — NO_CHANGE
IQVIA CEO cited "over 400 AI agents deployed to date" at Q3 results (2026-10-27) [VENDOR-CLAIM, secondary relay, count only]. Thermo Fisher and Danaher releases show no AI deployment.

### Banks — NO_CHANGE
- Bank of America (BAC), Q3 release: "more than 200,000 teammates using AI tools and 300-plus approved use cases"; headcount 209,911 against 213,384 a year earlier [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. No AI-linked saving is disclosed.
- Wells Fargo: noninterest expense down 1%, citing an 8% headcount reduction; the release does not attribute it to AI [VERIFIED-PUBLIC].
Evidence covers about 28.6% of cap. JPMorgan's CFO said the bank is moving away from quantifying AI benefits.

### Diversified Financials — NO_CHANGE
Schwab call: developer productivity up about 25%; clients trading via LLMs/APIs "doubled in about the last six months" [VENDOR-CLAIM, management statement via transcript host]. Mastercard's $202M year-to-date restructuring charge is not attributed to AI.

### Insurance — NO_CHANGE
Chubb Q3: loss and loss-expense ratio 58.7% against 56.7% in the P&C segment; no AI mention. Travelers: in a claims pilot, more than 80% of claimants offered the tool engaged with it; no loss-adjustment-expense effect quantified [VENDOR-CLAIM, pilot]. Nothing ties AI to the loss side yet.

### Cloud & AI Platforms — DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL

The release does not attribute this to AI specifically; the AI link is [INFERRED].

### Enterprise Software (aggregate) — DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL
- ServiceNow (NOW), Q3 2026: revenue $4,106M (+20.5%); 2026 AI annual contract value target raised to $1.7B from $1B; agentic AI consumption in production up more than 13x year on year (a usage metric, not revenue) [VERIFIED-PUBLIC, company metric].
- SAP: cloud revenue €6,668M (+26%), current cloud backlog €24,144M (+28%); headcount rose to 113,076 [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. The AI link is [INFERRED].
IBM consulting was flat. Salesforce, Adobe and Intuit had not reported.

### Enterprise Software — AI-Native — NO_CHANGE
Private companies; context only. A Cognition revenue run-rate of $1B reported by an aggregator could not be confirmed as company-disclosed, so it is not scored.

### Enterprise Software — Legacy — NO_CHANGE
Workday 8-K (2026-09-29): about 2.5% workforce reduction, $65–80M charges; the filing does not mention AI [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. HubSpot's CEO said its cuts were "not driven by AI-related efficiencies". No seat-compression disclosure from a primary filing.

### Technology Hardware — NO_CHANGE (thin)
Seagate (1.8% of cap): quarter revenue $4.2B, up 61%, data center about 81% of sales [INFERRED: trade-press relay of the company release]. Apple (63% of cap) reports 2026-11-02; no evidence this month.

### Semiconductor Equipment & Foundry — DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL
- Lam Research: September-quarter revenue $8.22B; December-quarter guide $8.60B ±$0.4B [VERIFIED-PUBLIC].
- TSMC (US$46.72B revenue, HPC 68%, 2026 capex US$60–64B reiterated) and ASML (€11.7B sales, 2026 guide €44–45B) [INFERRED: secondary relays; TSMC dollar figure is a conversion].
About 81% of cap covered; KLA and Applied Materials were not checked.

### Fabless Chips & Memory — DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL (low coverage)
- Micron, fiscal Q4 (ended 2026-09-03): revenue $54.23B against $41.46B the prior quarter; fiscal Q1 guide $61.5B ±$1.5B [VERIFIED-PUBLIC].
- Intel, Q3: revenue $16.5B (+21%); data-center and AI segment $7.3B (+78%) [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. The release gives no AI-specific attribution; the link is [INFERRED].
Covers about 12.6% of cap; NVIDIA (44%) reports 2026-11-17.

### Telecom — NO_CHANGE
Verizon signed a $2.0B+ agreement with AWS for infrastructure for agentic and generative AI workloads (commercial agreement, not AI deployed inside the company) [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. AT&T and T-Mobile Q3 releases show no AI deployment.

### Media & Entertainment — NO_CHANGE
Netflix cut about 6% of its workforce; the Q3 letter cites roughly 120 basis points of fourth-quarter margin pressure and contains no AI statement [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. The cuts are not filed as AI displacement.

### Interactive Media — DEPLOYMENT_ACCEL
- Alphabet (GOOGL/GOOG, 81% of cap; Waymo is a private Alphabet unit, context only), Q3: Google Cloud revenue $30.7B (against $15.2B); Cloud operating income $11.1B (against $3.6B); capex $51.4B (against $24.0B); Gemini app one billion users; headcount 204,757 (against 190,167) [VERIFIED-PUBLIC].
- Meta (17% of cap), Q3: revenue $64.60B (+26%); capex $34.91B; 2026 capex guide $135–145B; headcount 66,775, down 15% (not attributed to AI in the release) [VERIFIED-PUBLIC].

### Utilities — NO_CHANGE
NextEra Q3 (2026-10-21): 3.7 GW added to backlog (about 36.7 GW total) and definitive agreements for three federal hub projects [VERIFIED-PUBLIC]. This is large-load demand, not AI use inside the industry. The industry remains under regulatory review; no October ruling was found.

### Real Estate — NO_CHANGE
Data-center REIT demand reports (Equinix) could only be found on a secondary site and are not scored.
